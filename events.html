2022 VineStock

The 2022 VineStock music festival marked the return of this signature event for the first time since 2019 and featured an eclectic lineup of local musicians and vendors gathering at Georgian Hills Vineyards to raise money for South Georgian Bay area school music programs.

With the help of our amazing sponsors and the generosity of all guests in attendance, we raised an astounding $8,300 for South Georgian Bay Music Foundation!

2020 Bands Over the Bay!

The Mike McCarthy Band

The South Georgian Bay Music Foundation brought back the summer concert by putting the cart before the lawn chair. Together with sponsors, they put on the “world’s first-ever golf cart, physically-distanced, Bands Over the Bay,” on Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2020.

The show was held at Duntroon Highlands Golf Club featuring a live performance by The Mike McCarthy Band, and the audience was seated and separated in golf carts.

CHS Productions provided the stage and sound for The Mike McCarthy Band performance, and an anonymous “major donor” kicked in support along with Monaco and Georgian Bay Spirit Company. They all support music, arts, and culture in our area — thank you, it was a spectacular summer evening.

The South Georgian Bay Music Foundation hosts regular events through the year, including Jazz and Blues at the Station, to raise money for area school music programs. At the time, the foundation had recently donated $15,000 to high school and elementary school music programs. The money raised at Bands Over the Bay went toward the same effort.

With special thanks to Duncan Bristow for doing such a wonderful job filming the concert so we can share it with you.

2020 Bands Over the Bay!

Motown and Marley

Later that summer, on Sept. 16, 2020, Bands Over the Bay returned with Motown and Marley — another physically distanced concert supporting South Georgian Bay Music Foundation and local school music programs.
