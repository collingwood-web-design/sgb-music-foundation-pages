Our goal

The South Georgian Bay Music Foundation supports music education in our area schools.

Music education has an impact on all learning. It makes children smarter and stimulates their brains, requiring them to simultaneously listen for the beat, read along with sheet music, and coordinate their movements to play the instrument or dance with the rhythm.

Thank you!

2025 donations from Jazz and Blues at The Station 2025 have been distributed to school principals to be specifically used for music programs at local South Georgian Bay schools. We help area schools with instruments, instrument repairs, sheet music, instruction, a music scholarship and visits by professional musicians.

We donated to music programs at South Georgian Bay secondary and elementary schools.

  • Our Lady of The Bay CHS
  • Collingwood Collegiate Institute
  • Stayner Collegiate Institute
  • Georgian Bay Community School in Meaford
  • St. Mary’s Elementary
  • Cameron Street Elementary
