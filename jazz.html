2026 Jazz and Blues LIVE at the Station

South Georgian Bay Music Foundation is delighted to present this summer’s music series: Jazz & Blues Live at The Station.

  • June 24 – Jimmy and the Extractions
  • July 8 – Johannes Linstead Band
  • July 15 – Jazz Connexion Big Band Cancelled due to air quality
  • July 22 – Rosie’s Smokehouse Deluxe
  • July 29 – Boomerang
  • August 5 – The Mike McCarthy Band
  • August 12 – Jazz Connexion Big Band Rescheduled
Subscribe for next season Support with a donation

What, when, where

What: An all ages / family friendly, FREE and accessible music series

When: 6 – 8pm, June 24, July 8, July 15, July 22, July 29, Aug. 5, Aug 12

Where: The Station Park, Collingwood Museum, 45 St. Paul Street, Collingwood ON

Cost: FREE and accessible! No admission is charged. Donations are encouraged

What to bring: Please bring your lawn chair, camping chair or blanket to sit on at the concerts and don’t forget to bring your dancing shoes.

Collingwood Ice Cream Co. will be providing cool summer treats, beer & snacks from Sidelaunch Brewing.

Parking Reminder

Please be considerate when parking.

If using the DunnCap parking lot, please leave the reserved tenant spaces and the two rows between the light poles on the Museum side available.

Thank you for helping us maintain this valued community partnership.

Limited parking is available at the Collingwood Leisure Time Club. If you park there, please be respectful of members and others using the facility.

New Rain Venue

Side Launch Brewing Company

We are happy to announce that Jazz and Blues LIVE at the Station has a NEW rain venue!

In the event of bad weather on a Wednesday evening, the show will move to the Side Launch Brewing Company, 200 Mountain Rd Unit 1, Collingwood, ON, L9Y 4V5

Stay up to date by signing up for our newsletter to receive updates directly in your inbox. You can also follow us on social media, and listen for announcements on 95.1 The Peak FM on the day of the show.

Who

2026 Band bios & links

June 24
Jimmy and the Extractions

Read bioHide bio

Jimmy & The Extractions are longtime friends and band mates living in the Southern Georgian Bay area. They all love playing Canadiana, Alternative, Rock and Folk-Roots-Rock music. Inspired by Canadian bands such as Blue Rodeo and The Tragically Hip, the Extractions often add a rootsy feel to the contemporary hit songs they play. What sets this band apart is their vocal power. Tackling intricate harmonies is a joy for these individuals. This band has chemistry and it’s infectious! From the first song to the last, you’ll be swept up by the positive energy the Extractions embrace.

Lead Vocals & Guitar: Jim Harrison Guitar, Mandolin & Vocals: Marilyn Reid Keyboards & Vocals: Samantha Moneypenny Bass & Vocals: Lenny Fligg Drums & Vocals: Don Reid

July 8
Johannes Linstead

Read bioHide bio

500 Million Streams. 8 “Best Album” Awards. 1 Unforgettable Night. Experience Johannes Linstead, Canada’s “Guitarist of the Year” and international Latin Jazz sensation, live on stage! In his spectacular new show, “From Spain to Cuba,” Johannes unleashes a fiery musical journey that bridges the exotic Mediterranean with the pulsing rhythms of the Caribbean. Backed by a world-class band delivering sizzling Latin percussion, deep grooves, and soulful accordion, his virtuosic Spanish guitar mastery sets the stage on fire. From explosive, upbeat rumbas to deeply moving ballads, this is a vibrant, multi-cultural celebration of sound that will leave you breathless.

July 22
Rosie’s Smokehouse Deluxe

Read bioHide bio

Rosie’s Smokehouse Deluxe. A powerhouse Roots Rock band hailing from the shores of Ontario Canada’s Southern Georgian Bay. The band is led by founding member, lead vocalist & bass guitarist David G. Rose, aka: Rosie. With the release of their debut album “Welcome To The Smokehouse”, the band burst onto the music scene garnering rave reviews both at home and around the globe. Rosie has assembled an unbelievably stellar lineup of seasoned veterans to help bring “Rosie’s Smokehouse Deluxe” to life. Their live show is an incredibly dynamic and powerful must see experience, pure fire from the drop of the beat.

July 29
Boomerang

Read bioHide bio

Boomerang invites you into a time machine to experience vintage classics from a golden era of soul/R&B music. For the past twenty years, this nine-piece horn band has gotten audiences on their feet, celebrating songs that inspired generations to turn down the lights and turn up the volume. Boomerang players have shared stages with many well-known artists over the years. We feature the great tunes from Stevie Wonder to Sam and Dave to Wilson Pickett to Chicago and Tower of Power and more. The rhythm section honours the best of studio musicians from Motown to Stax Records and brings the spirit of soul music that floated on the Motor City airwaves.

August 5
The Mike McCarthy Band

Read bioHide bio

The Mike McCarthy Band is a Canadian musical ensemble celebrated for its dynamic fusion of neo country, southern rock, and Canadiana influences. Led by the versatile singer-songwriter Mike McCarthy, the band delivers a captivating blend of heartfelt lyrics, gritty guitar riffs and soulful melodies that resonate with audiences far and wide. Erica Prinn-McCarthy adds harmonies that infuse their sound with additional depth and richness, seamlessly blending with Mike’s lead vocals to create captivating melodies that linger in the hearts of listeners. With a periodic rotating roster of seasoned and talented musicians, 2026 boasts Jerome Tucker on Bass, Carl Harvey on Lead Guitar, Gabor Szepesi on Organ and Keys and Rich Greenspoon on Drums. Doesn’t get any better than that! Each one of these amazing musicians stand alone in their own shining light. A hard driving yet melodic sound that’s irresistible to the ear and to dancing feet, the band defies categorization while remaining accessible to all who appreciate authentic, heartfelt music.

Rescheduled — Aug. 12
Jazz Connexion Big Band

Read bioHide bio

Jazz Connexion Big Band is a 9-piece ensemble bringing timeless classic jazz to life through original arrangements of cool jazz standards and jazzy versions of contemporary tunes. Featuring a mix of vocal and instrumental performances, the band, led by Gary Chin (Music Director and various instruments) and David Heaslip (vocals) is composed of outstanding Southern Georgian Bay area musicians who share a passion for playing and preserving this rich musical genre tradition.

Left to Right: Doug Collier, bass guitar Jill Morrison, trombone Richard Uglow, keyboards & guitar Sandy Fair, tenor sax Peter Montgomery, baritone sax Susan Connelly, alto sax David Heaslip, vocals Gary Chin, music director, trombone, guitar, bass, drums also: Paul Thompson, trumpet & flugelhorn
